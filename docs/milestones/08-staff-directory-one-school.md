# Milestone 08 — Staff directory and one-school setup

**Finished:** 2026-10-02 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard (all staff), server

## Goal

Help staff reach each other quickly, and shape the product around a single school, which is the product direction.

## What was delivered

### Staff directory and presence
- **Coordinators and Manager pages for teachers, and a Manager page for coordinators.**
  - Each shows the person's role, phone and email.
  - Each has a one-tap Message button.
- **Online status.** Staff can see who is online right now.
- **Message notifications.** New messages appear in the bell, as a pop-up alert and as unread badges.
- **Safe with several browser tabs open.** Signing out in one tab signs out everywhere, and two accounts never mix in one browser.

### Admin and leadership messaging
- The Admin can message any staff member or parent directly.
- Coordinators can message teachers.
- Managers can start a conversation with a parent from the child's page.
- Complaints can be raised to a teacher, a coordinator or a manager.

### One school
- The product now serves **one school**. Multi-school screens and the school switcher are hidden, and the admin manages only their own school.
- **School logo.** Upload once, and it appears on the dashboard and in the app.
- **Weekly plan as PDF.**
- **First-day polish.** Clear first-open screens, the guided tour and technical views removed, and tabs that switch without reloading.

## Business value

- Faster internal communication, so issues are solved the same day.
- A simpler product that is easier to sell, train and support for a single school.
- The school's own brand is visible to every family.

## Who benefits

All staff communicate faster, and the school owner gets a focused, single-school product.
