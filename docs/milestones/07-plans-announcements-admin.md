# Milestone 07 — Weekly plans, announcements and school administration

**Finished:** 2026-10-01 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard (manager, admin, teacher, coordinator), mobile app (parent)

## Goal

Make the weekly plan and school news easy to publish and easy to read, and give the Admin everything needed to run the school's accounts.

## What was delivered

### Dashboard: manager
- **Weekly plans for each grade.**
  - The manager uploads one image or PDF per grade per week.
  - A "this week" overview, an archive of past weeks, replace and CSV export.
- **Announcements and events.** Sent to a grade or a whole department.
- **Phone numbers and a direct Message button.** On the Coordinators, Teachers and Children pages.
- **Messages with teachers, and School usage.**
- **A simpler menu.** Pages the manager does not need were removed.

### Dashboard: teachers and coordinators
- A Weekly plans tab, so everyone sees the same plan the parents see.

### Dashboard: admin
- **Home.** Counts for managers, coordinators, teachers, children, classes, workers and this week's lessons.
- **Teachers, Coordinators and Managers.**
  - Add and edit staff, with phone numbers, subjects and department.
  - Reset passwords.
- **Workers page.** Non-teaching staff.
- **Children and parents.**
  - Add a child and create the parent's account in one step.
  - Search, edit, and reset the parent's password.
- **A simpler menu.** Technical pages were removed: feature flags, platform usage and platform settings.

### Mobile app: parent
- **Weekly plan page.** Image or PDF, with an archive. Plan images are stored on the phone for fast, low-data viewing.
- **Announcements page.**
- **Parent phone number** on the parent's profile.

## Business value

- Parents always know what is coming this week, with less confusion and fewer "what is the homework?" calls.
- The Admin can enrol a child and the parent in one step, which makes onboarding families faster.
- Leadership has direct contact details for every staff member at hand.

## Who benefits

The Admin runs the school's accounts. Managers publish plans and news. Parents stay informed.
