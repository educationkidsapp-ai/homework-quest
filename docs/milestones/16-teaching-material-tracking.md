# Milestone 16 — Teaching material tracking

**Finished:** 2026-10-16 · **Status:** live on QA, switch off until the school turns it on · **Area:** dashboard (Teacher, Admin, Manager, Coordinator, Floor supervisor), server

## Goal

Follow the weekly lesson preparation — دفتر التحضير — the way the school already follows attendance: who handed in
on time, who was late, who has not handed in at all.

## What was delivered

### For the teacher
- **One page with a row for every class and subject she teaches**, for this week and for next week.
- **She attaches the preparation as a PDF or a photograph**, with a note if she wants one, and can replace it until
  it has been reviewed.
- **A reminder before the deadline**, on the dashboard bell and on her phone, naming how much is still missing.

### For the leaders
- **A board with a row per teacher**, her counts, and each class and subject, with the status worked out from the
  deadline: handed in on time, late, missing, or reviewed.
- **Every leader sees exactly her own people**, the same scope as the attendance board, limited to the staff who
  teach.
- **She opens the file and marks it reviewed with a comment.** A row is reviewed once.
- **The board downloads as a CSV.**

### For the Admin
- **She sets the deadline** — the weekday, the time, and how long before it the reminder goes out — on the
  Attendance settings page, in the school's own time zone.
- **Next week's material is due during this week**, so a change of deadline re-reads every row rather than freezing
  old verdicts.

## Business value

- Preparation is followed weekly without anyone collecting notebooks.
- A late or missing preparation is visible to the right leader on the day it happens, not at the end of term.
- The reviewed comment leaves a record of the feedback a teacher was actually given.

## Who benefits

Teachers hand in from where they are, in two taps. Coordinators, floor supervisors, managers and the Admin see the
week at a glance and keep a trail of what they reviewed.
