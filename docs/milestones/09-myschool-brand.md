# Milestone 09 — MySchool brand, logo colours and dark mode

**Finished:** 2026-10-02 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard and mobile app

## Goal

Give the product one recognisable name and look, and make it comfortable to use day or night.

## What was delivered

- **New name.** The product is now **MySchool** (Arabic: **مدرستي**), on the dashboard, in the app, on the app icon and in the browser title.
- **Logo colour palette.**
  - Built from the logo: a blue gradient, magenta and orange.
  - Used across both the dashboard and the mobile app.
  - The school's main colour was set to the logo blue.
- **Dark mode.**
  - Available on both the dashboard and the app.
  - In the app, the parent can choose Light, Dark or System.
  - Colour pairs are checked automatically to stay readable for accessibility in both modes.
- **Formal app design.** One consistent design language on every screen of the app, including lessons and exams.
- **Layout polish from the owner's list.** For example:
  - Add buttons in page headers.
  - A one-line Copy button.
  - Cleaner dialogs.
  - Consistent spacing and table borders.
  - A tidier side menu.

## Business value

- A consistent brand builds trust with parents and makes the product look professional in demos and app stores.
- Dark mode and readable colours improve comfort and accessibility.
- One design across web and mobile lowers training and support effort.

## Who benefits

The school's image with parents improves, and every user gets a more comfortable experience.
