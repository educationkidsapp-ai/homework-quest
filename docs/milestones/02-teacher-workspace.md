# Milestone 02 — Teacher workspace and lesson builder

**Finished:** 2026-09-23 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard (teacher), mobile app (child)

## Goal

Give every teacher one place to plan the week, manage their classes, and turn their own teaching material into interactive lessons for children.

## What was delivered

### Dashboard: teacher
- **This week.** A weekly calendar of the teacher's lessons across all their classes:
  - Search.
  - Drag a lesson to move it, or copy it to another day.
  - Non-teaching days are blocked.
- **My classes.** Each class has a page with tabs for Calendar, Children, Attendance, Gradebook and Exams.
- **Lesson builder from the teacher's own material.**
  1. The teacher uploads slides, a PDF or a photo of a page.
  2. The system reads it, including text recognition for photos.
  3. The system builds a lesson of short "stops", each with a question, a picture and a tip for the parent.
- **Edit in plain language.** Teachers change a stop by typing what they want. No technical knowledge is needed.
- **Publish to one class or several.** A lesson goes to all sibling classes in one step, and publishing can be undone.
- **Attendance.** Mark present or absent with notes.

### Mobile app: child
- Children play the published lessons as short, game-like steps.
- Question types include multiple choice, true or false, ordering, matching, fill the blank, write a sentence, number line, tracing, drawing and reading.

### Design
- A clean, professional dashboard design with comfortable reading sizes.
- Arabic (right to left) and English.
- A dark mode.

## Business value

- Teachers reuse material they already have instead of authoring from scratch, which saves hours each week.
- One view of the week reduces planning mistakes and clashes.
- Children get interactive practice that matches exactly what was taught in class.

## Who benefits

Teachers save time, and children get engaging practice tied to the classroom.
