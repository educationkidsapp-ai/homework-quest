# Milestone 06 — Coordinator and Manager roles

**Finished:** 2026-09-27 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard (coordinator, manager), mobile app (parent)

## Goal

Give the school's middle and senior leadership their own views. Subject coordinators oversee teaching quality. Department managers run their department.

## What was delivered

### Dashboard: coordinator (oversees one or more subjects)
- **Home.** A summary of the coordinator's subjects and classes.
- **Teachers.** The teachers in the coordinator's subjects and their class assignments.
- **Classes.** A monthly calendar of every class in scope.
- **Lessons, read only.** Filterable by status.
- **Attendance, Gradebook, Exam results and each child's page.** All read only, and limited to the coordinator's subjects.
- **Messages.** With parents, teachers, the manager and the admin.
- **Complaints inbox and announcements** to the parents of classes in scope.

### Dashboard: manager (runs a department)
- **Home statistics.** For each grade: children, attendance rate and exam averages.
- **Coordinators, Teachers and People directory.** People can be exported to CSV.
- **Staff attendance.** Daily marking for teachers and coordinators, with a monthly summary.
- **Broadcasts.** Weekly plans, announcements and events, sent to the whole department or a chosen audience, with an expiry date.
- **Messages.** With parents, teachers, coordinators and the admin.

### Mobile app: parent
- Conversations are grouped by staff role: teacher, coordinator, manager.
- The parent can message the subject coordinator or the department manager directly.
- **School news.** The weekly plan is pinned at the top, with announcements and events below it, marked as read once seen.

## Business value

- Leaders see real data about attendance, results and lesson status without asking teachers for reports.
- Each person sees only their own department or subjects, which protects privacy and focus.
- One channel for school-wide news replaces paper notes and scattered messages.

## Who benefits

- Coordinators: oversight of teaching quality.
- Managers: an overview of their department and the means to communicate with it.
- Parents: one trusted news feed.
