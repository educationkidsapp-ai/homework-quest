# Milestone 10 — Parent and child mobile app

**Finished:** 2026-10-03 · **Status:** Android on testers' phones (Firebase App Distribution). iPhone is built but not yet on a device · **Area:** mobile app

## Goal

Give every family one app: the child learns, and the parent follows, communicates and stays informed.

## What was delivered

### Parent
- **Sign in, and the children appear automatically.**
  - Children come from the school's records, so the parent does not have to add them.
  - The parent can switch between children.
- **App lock.** Face ID, fingerprint or the phone's screen lock, offered after the first sign-in. The parent area is protected even when the child is using the phone.
- **Parent home.** The child's lessons and progress.
- **Progress.** Skill levels, released exam results with the score, and teacher comments.
- **Weekly plan, School news and Messages.** Messages reach the teacher, coordinator, manager and the school administration.
- **Notifications tab** with badges.
- **Today widget on the home screen**, and an exam countdown in the notification area.
- **Settings.** Language, Light, Dark or System theme, and the app lock.
- **Parent tips** for each lesson.

### Child
- **Child home.** Today's and the week's lessons by subject, plus exam cards with their opening and closing times.
- **Many question types.** Multiple choice, true or false, ordering, matching, fill the blank, write a sentence, number line, tracing, drawing, reading and the exit ticket.
- **Levels with hints and stars.**
- **Read-aloud** in Arabic, French or English, matching the lesson's language.
- **Exams.**
  - One sitting, with a neutral "Answer saved" after every answer and no hints.
  - The exam resumes if the child leaves.
  - A countdown timer.
  - Answers given offline are sent later.
- **After an exam.** A Submitted screen first, then the result once the teacher releases it.

### Quality
- Six complete parent journeys were tested end to end, and all 13 issues found were fixed. The journeys were: notification, weekly plan, event, exam round trip, complaints, and messaging.

## Business value

- One app per family keeps the school present at home every day.
- Children practise in a safe, distraction-free space. Parents stay informed without calling the school.
- Fair exams at home, using the same rules as in class.

## Who benefits

Children, through engaging practice. Parents, through visibility and contact with the school. The school, through stronger family engagement.

## Not yet done

- Face ID has not yet been tested on a real iPhone.
