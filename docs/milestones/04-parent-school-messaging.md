# Milestone 04 — Parent and school messaging

**Finished:** 2026-09-25 · **Status:** live on QA · **Area:** dashboard (all staff), mobile app (parent)

## Goal

Give parents and the school one private, real-time channel instead of scattered phone calls and WhatsApp groups.

## What was delivered

- **Live chat between parents and teachers.** Messages appear instantly on both sides.
  - Staff use the dashboard's Messages page.
  - Parents use the app's Messages tab.
- **One conversation per child and teacher.** Each conversation stays in context, so the history is never mixed up.
- **Notification bell in the dashboard.**
  - Staff see new events: a new message, a lesson ready, and more.
  - Unread counts are shown.
- **Built for scale.** Messages reach the right person even when the school's traffic is spread across several servers.

## Business value

- All school-parent communication is in one auditable place that the school owns.
- Teachers keep their private phone numbers private.
- Parents get answers faster, which builds trust in the school.

## Who benefits

Parents get easy access to teachers. Teachers keep work separate from personal channels. Leadership gets a record of communication.
